Tradition & Government Ratification

The Ascension of Òsómẹ̀kùn I

Following the peaceful transition of the revered monarch, the late Oba Samuel Atanda Oladipo Ayerowunwi, a rigorous, transparent, and traditional screening was carried out by the accredited kingmakers of Ifo Land.

"In strict observance of ancient Yoruba royal traditions and statutory constitutional protocols, Kabiyesi formally relinquished all honorary chieftaincy titles to ascend solely to the stool of his ancestors as Oba Olumide Atanda John Osunsina (Òsómẹ̀kùn I)."

The appointment was unanimously endorsed by the traditional institution and officially ratified by the Executive Council of the Ogun State Government, heralding a golden epoch for all sons, daughters, and residents of Ifoland.

Pedigree & Statesmanship

From Builder of Nations to Royal Father

Before ascending the royal throne, Oba Olumide Osunsina made indelible marks as a visionary industrialist, philanthropist, and founder/CEO of Megamound Investment Limited—pioneering iconic residential master-planned developments and transforming Nigeria’s urban real estate landscape for over three decades.

Through the Olumide Osunsina Foundation, he has impacted thousands of lives across education, scholarships, healthcare outreaches, and youth vocational skills. Kabiyesi brings this unprecedented wealth of administrative excellence to the traditional governance of Ifo Land.

Academic Excellence

Early Life & Discipline

Born in 1963 in Abeokuta, Ogun State, his passion for property development was nurtured early by the legacy of his late mother, Madam Folasade Osunsina, an astute real estate investor.

  • • Barewa College, Zaria & Abeokuta Grammar School (Division 1, 1978).
  • • B.Sc. in Building Technology from Ahmadu Bello University (ABU), Zaria.
  • • Advanced Diploma in Management Studies (with Distinction) from the University of Lagos (UNILAG).
  • • Master of Business Administration (MBA) from Abubakar Tafawa Balewa University (ATBU), Bauchi.
  • • Advanced executive training in corporate leadership in the United Kingdom.
🏗️

The Megamound Legacy

Visionary Real Estate Titan

After building expertise in finance, dredging, and engineering services, he incorporated Megamound Investment Limited in 1992, steering it into a multi-billion-naira conglomerate.

  • • Carlton Gate Estate (Chevron Drive, Lekki, Lagos) — a 28.7-hectare flagship reclamation & residential community.
  • • Lekky County Homes (Ikota, Lekki) — a premier master-planned estate.
  • • Expansion of Carlton Gate into Ibadan (Oyo State) and Abeokuta (Ogun State).
  • • King’s Court and multi-sector corporate board directorships including IPWA Plc.
🤝

Philanthropic Impact

Empowering Generations

Through the Olumide Osunsina Foundation (OOF), Kabiyesi has consistently championed social welfare, youth empowerment, and economic independence across Southwestern Nigeria.

  • • Annual university and secondary school scholarship funds for underserved students.
  • • Vocational technical training centers providing skills in technology, craft, and entrepreneurship.
  • • Extensive medical outreach clinics, elderly support, and community infrastructure aid.
👑

Ascension to the Throne

Òsómẹ̀kùn I of Ifoland

Prior to his royal accession, he held prestigious honorary chieftaincies including Asoju Oba of Ifoland, Asiwaju Bobagunwa of Egbaland, and Otunba.

  • • Formally surrendered all honorary titles in strict compliance with Yoruba customs to ascend the sacred stool.
  • • Unanimously endorsed by the accredited Kingmakers of Ifo Land.
  • • Officially approved and ratified in the Ogun State Government Gazette in September 2026.

Official Gazette Ogun State Ministry of Local Government & Chieftaincy Affairs

Formal Ratification & Presentation of Royal Staff of Office

The Executive Council of Ogun State officially announces the approval and certification of His Royal Majesty, Oba Olumide Atanda John Osunsina as the substantive Olu of Ifo Land, reigning under the traditional regnal title Òsómẹ̀kùn I.

"Let the peace and divine wisdom of the Almighty guide our Kabiyesi as he sits on the throne of our ancestors. May his reign usher in unbounded prosperity, longevity, and unity throughout the kingdom."

All traditional councils, baales, community leaders, and the general public are officially notified that all customary prerogatives and authorities of the royal stool of Ifo Land are vested in Kabiyesi Òsómẹ̀kùn I.
