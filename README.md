# 👑 Official Royal Web Portal
### His Royal Majesty, Alayeluwa Oba Olumide Atanda John Osunsina (Òsómẹ̀kùn I)
**The Olu of Ifo Land, Ogun State, Nigeria**

---

## 🏛️ Overview
This repository contains the official, commemorative web portal for **His Royal Majesty, Oba Olumide Atanda John Osunsina (Òsómẹ̀kùn I)**, celebrating his accession to the ancient royal stool of Ifo Land following his unanimous endorsement by the kingmakers and official ratification by the Ogun State Government.

---

## ✨ Features
- **Official Royal Heraldry & Crests**: High-resolution, vector-isolated royal coat of arms and circular seals extracted directly from the official palace gazette PDF.
- **Dual-Theme Presentation**: Instant switcher between the **Imperial Gold Edition** and the **Royal Navy Edition** with real-time accent shifts.
- **Progressive Scroll System**:
  - Top-pinned glowing reading progress meter.
  - Interactive circular SVG progress gauge and percentage badge integrated into the permanent lower-right royal seal.
  - IntersectionObserver cascading fade and slide reveals for sections and cards.
  - Parallax tracking on the central hero crest.
- **The 4 Pillars of Vision**: Outlining Kabiyesi’s strategic blueprint for Ifo Land across Urban Modernization, Youth Empowerment, Industrial Commerce, and Cultural Heritage.
- **Palace News & Press Media Kit**: Official announcements, coronation decree, and direct one-click downloads for high-res transparent brand assets.
- **Interactive Royal Proclamation**: Modal verification certificate accessible from the navigation bar, hero actions, or by clicking the floating seal.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
- **Core**: Semantic HTML5 & Vanilla ES6+ JavaScript.
- **Styling**: Vanilla CSS3 with custom design tokens, fluid typography, and glassmorphism.
- **Typography**: Google Fonts (*Cinzel*, *Cormorant Garamond*, *Plus Jakarta Sans*).
- **Zero Heavy Dependencies**: Lightweight, lightning-fast load times, and fully responsive across mobile, tablet, and desktop devices.

## 🚀 Live Deployment via GitHub Pages
To publish this portal live on the web for free using GitHub Pages:
1. Navigate to your repository settings: **Settings** → **Pages**.
2. Under **Build and deployment** → **Source**, select **Deploy from a branch**.
3. Choose branch: `main` and folder: `/ (root)`, then click **Save**.
4. Your royal portal will be live in minutes at:  
   👉 `https://webtrovert0x.github.io/osomekun/`
